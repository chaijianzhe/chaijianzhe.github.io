About me

My research focuses on reliable evaluation of large language models and AI security. I study benchmark contamination, inference-time decontamination, and the detection of AI-generated content, with an interest in distinguishing genuine model capabilities from memorization.

I am a PhD student in Sakuma Lab at the Institute of Science Tokyo and a Research Associate in the AI Security and Privacy Team at RIKEN AIP. I also work as a part-time Research Assistant at the Institute of Science Tokyo, where I completed my M.S. in September 2026.

I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC) in June 2024.

Publications

When Benchmarks Leak: Inference-Time Decontamination for LLMs

Jianzhe Chai, Yu Zhe, Jun Sakuma

ACL, Main Conference, 2026

We introduce DeconIEP, an inference-time framework that reduces the influence of benchmark memorization using small, bounded perturbations in the input embedding space. It preserves the evaluation set while targeting contamination-driven performance inflation.
